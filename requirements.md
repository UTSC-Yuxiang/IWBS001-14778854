# The Corner Cafe Website Requirements

## 1. Project overview

The Corner Cafe is a small family cafe run by Tom and Maria near a train station. Its customers are mainly commuters and local office workers. The cafe does not currently have a website. Customers want to check the menu before visiting and quickly find the cafe's location and opening hours.

The website should feel warm and friendly. It should be simple to use and should help customers find the information they need without unnecessary features.

## 2. Client goals

Help customers find the cafe and check its opening hours.
Show food and drink choices with clear, easy-to-read prices.
Make the website welcoming and consistent with the cafe's atmosphere.
Make the website easy to use on mobile phones, tablets and laptops.
Make the weekly specials easy for the cafe to update each Monday.

## 3. Website pages and content

### Home

Include a welcoming introduction to The Corner Cafe.
Include a suitable cafe or coffee photo.
Show the opening hours clearly on a phone screen without requiring the visitor to scroll.
Provide clear navigation to the other three pages.

### Menu

Show food and drinks in separate, clearly labelled sections: Breakfast, Lunch and Drinks.
Show a price for each menu item.
Align prices neatly so customers can compare items easily.
Make the menu easy to read on a mobile phone.

### Weekly Specials

Include a clearly visible area for the current weekly special.
Make the special stand out from the other page content.
Organise the page so Maria can update the special each Monday without changing the whole page.

### Find Us

Display the cafe's address.
Include a map image or an embedded map.
Display the opening hours.
Include contact details so customers can contact the cafe.

## 4. Requirements checklist

The following requirements can be checked against the finished website.

The website has four pages: Home, Menu, Weekly Specials and Find Us. All four pages exist and can be opened in a browser.
A clear navigation menu appears on every page. Each page shows the navigation menu, and every navigation link opens the correct page.
Opening hours appear on every page. The opening hours can be found on all four pages, for example in a consistent footer.
The Home page includes a welcome message, a cafe/coffee photo and opening hours visible on a phone without scrolling. At a phone-sized screen width, the welcome message, photo area and opening hours are visible when the Home page first loads.
The Menu page contains Breakfast, Lunch and Drinks sections, with prices. All three sections are present and each listed menu item has a price.
Menu prices line up neatly and the menu is easy to read on a phone. Prices are consistently aligned, text is readable, and the menu does not require sideways scrolling on a phone.
The Weekly Specials page highlights the current special and is easy to update. A visitor can easily identify the special, and its content can be changed without redesigning the page.
The Find Us page includes an address, map and contact details. The page displays an address, a map image or embedded map, and contact information.
The website works on phone, tablet and laptop screens. Content and navigation remain usable at phone, tablet and laptop screen sizes, without unwanted horizontal scrolling.
The website uses a warm and friendly visual style. Colours, images and page layouts create a welcoming cafe feel and do not look overly corporate.
Every image has alternative text (alt text). Each content image includes meaningful alt text in its HTML.
Text is readable and colour contrast is sufficient. Text can be read clearly, and text/background colours have enough contrast.

## 5. Client preferences and answers from Week 8

 Visual style:Warm, friendly and welcoming.
Preferred colours: Pink, orange and yellow.
Photos: The client agreed that cafe/coffee photos can be used.
Recorded opening hours: Weekdays 6:30 am to 3:00 pm; weekends 7:00 am to 2:00 pm.
Design decision:Use a warm colour palette based on the client's preferred pink, orange and yellow, with clear text and enough contrast for readability.

## 6. Responsive design and accessibility

Use a layout that adjusts to phone, tablet and laptop screen sizes.
Keep navigation easy to find and use on every page.
Use readable text sizes and clear headings.
Use sufficient colour contrast, including when using pink, orange and yellow.
Add meaningful alt text to every image.
Make sure the menu can be read on a phone without sideways scrolling.

## 7. Out of scope

The Week 8 worksheet identifies the following features as outside the client's request. They are not included in the planned website:

Online ordering and online payment.
Customer accounts or a membership system.
Food delivery service.
Table booking system.
